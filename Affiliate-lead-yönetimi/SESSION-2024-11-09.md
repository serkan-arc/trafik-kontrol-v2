   - Integrated automatic commission calculation

#### **Komisyon Mantığı Detayları:**

**CPL (Cost Per Lead):**
```typescript
Valid statuses: approved_for_crm, sent_to_crm, contacted, approved, shipped, delivered, sold
Commission: Fixed amount from deal
```

**CPA (Cost Per Action):**
```typescript
Valid statuses: sold, delivered
Commission: Fixed amount from deal
```

**CPS (Cost Per Sale):**
```typescript
Valid status: sold
Requires: sale_amount
Commission: (sale_amount × percentage) / 100
```

**HYBRID (Lead + Sale):**
```typescript
Lead sent: Lead commission (fixed)
Sale completed: Sale commission (fixed) - separate commission
```

**REVSHARE:**
```typescript
Placeholder for monthly revenue sharing
Requires: Monthly job for recurring calculations
```

- ✅ Status webhook → Automatic commission trigger
- ✅ Duplicate prevention → One commission per lead
- ✅ Response integration → Commission results in API responses
